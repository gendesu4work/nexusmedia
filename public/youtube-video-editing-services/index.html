Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a YouTube video editor?

Nexus Media charges under 25 USD per hour, compared with about 40 to 100+ USD per hour for editors in the US, Canada and the UK. A 10 to 15 minute YouTube video usually takes 4 to 8 hours to edit, depending on B-roll, graphics and captions.

Can you turn one long video or podcast into Shorts?

Yes. We cut the strongest moments from your long-form videos and podcasts into vertical Shorts, Reels and TikToks with captions.

Can you match my existing editing style?

Yes. Send a few videos you like and we match the pacing, captions, zooms and sound design.

How do I send footage?

Upload your files to Google Drive, Dropbox or any download link. We send the finished edit back online, ready to upload.

Do you upload to my channel?

If you want us to, yes. You can add us as a channel manager in YouTube Studio, and you keep full ownership of the channel.
