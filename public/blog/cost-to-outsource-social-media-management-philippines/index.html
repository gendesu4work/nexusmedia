How much does it cost to outsource social media management to the Philippines?

What a monthly social media package from a Philippine agency includes, how it compares with US, UK and Australian agencies, and what to budget for ads.

By the Nexus Media team, Manila · Updated November 21, 2026

Short answer

Philippine social media agencies typically charge under 25 USD per hour, so a full monthly package (strategy, about 30 designed posts and reels, community management and a report) usually costs a fraction of what US, UK or Australian agencies charge, which is often 2,000 to 10,000+ USD a month. At Nexus Media, most monthly packages start from about 1,000 USD. Your ad spend on Meta or TikTok is paid separately, directly to the platform.

What a monthly package usually includes

  • Content strategy and a monthly content calendar
  • Post, carousel and reel design (often around 30 pieces a month)
  • Captions, hashtags and scheduling
  • Community management: replying to comments and messages, usually within 24 hours
  • Paid ads setup and optimisation on Meta and TikTok (management fee only)
  • A monthly analytics report with what worked and what to change

Typical monthly hours

PackageWhat's in itTypical hours a month
Starter1–2 platforms, 12–15 posts, light community management20–35
Growth2–3 platforms, about 30 posts and reels, daily community management, report40–70
Growth + adsGrowth package plus Meta and TikTok ad campaigns and creative testing60–100

At under 25 USD per hour, a Growth package lands around 1,000 to 1,750 USD a month. The same hours at a 75 USD local agency rate would be roughly 3,000 to 5,250 USD.

Ad spend is separate

The agency fee pays for the work. Your ad budget goes straight to Meta or TikTok on your own card, inside your own ad account. Small businesses often start testing with about 10 to 30 USD a day and scale what works. Be wary of anyone who wants to hold your ad budget for you or run ads from an account you don't own.

Why brands outsource social media to the Philippines

  • English and culture: the Philippines is one of the most active social media countries in the world, and Filipino teams write naturally for US, UK and Australian audiences.
  • Coverage: with Manila at UTC+8, comments and DMs that arrive overnight in the US or Europe get answered while you sleep.
  • One team: the same agency can design posts, edit reels and run ads, instead of hiring three freelancers.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. Who owns the accounts, the ad account and the content files? (You should.)
  2. How many posts, reels and stories are included each month, exactly?
  3. Is reel editing included or charged separately?
  4. How fast are comments and messages answered, and on which days?
  5. What is in the monthly report, and who walks you through it?
