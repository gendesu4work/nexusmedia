Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to outsource video editing?

Yes. Most of our clients abroad send footage at the end of their day and wake up to finished edits, because Manila is 12 to 16 hours ahead of North America. We reply to messages within 24 hours.

Do you offer white-label video editing for agencies?

Yes. Marketing agencies and production companies can outsource client edits to us and deliver them under their own brand. We work only with you and never contact your clients.

Why outsource video editing to the Philippines?

Filipino video editors deliver the same professional quality at much lower rates than editors in the US, UK, Canada or Australia, with fluent English communication. Because Manila is ahead of the US and Europe, edits are often done while you sleep.

Is Nexus Media a video editing agency based in the Philippines?

Yes. Nexus Media is a digital agency based in the Philippines. Our video editing team works fully online, so we can edit for clients anywhere in the Philippines and abroad.

What kinds of videos do you edit?

Short-form reels and TikToks, YouTube long-form videos, video ads, brand films, podcast clips and stream highlights, including captions, motion graphics, colour grading and sound design.
