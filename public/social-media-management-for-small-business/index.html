Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost for a small business?

At Nexus Media, a full monthly package with strategy, about 30 posts and reels, community management and a report starts from about 1,000 USD. Many US, Canadian and UK agencies charge 2,000 to 10,000+ USD a month for the same scope. Ad spend is paid separately.

Is it cheaper than hiring a social media manager?

Usually, yes. A full-time social media manager in the US often costs 4,000+ USD a month before benefits. With us you get a team covering strategy, design, video and ads for a fraction of that.

What do you need from me?

Access to your accounts, your logo and brand colours, and photos or short phone videos of your products, place or team. We handle the rest and send a calendar for your approval each month.

Do I keep ownership of my accounts?

Yes. Your pages, ad accounts and content stay in your name. We get access as a partner or manager, never ownership.
