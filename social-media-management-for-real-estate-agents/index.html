Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost for a real estate agent?

At Nexus Media, a full monthly package with about 30 posts and reels, community management and a report starts from about 1,000 USD, compared with 2,000 to 10,000+ USD a month at many US, Canadian and UK agencies. Ad spend is paid separately.

Do you follow real estate advertising rules?

Yes. We set Meta ads up under the housing special ad category where required, and we follow the disclosure and branding details your brokerage gives us. Final compliance approval stays with you or your broker.

Can you run lead ads for buyers and sellers?

Yes. We plan and run Meta lead ads and can send new leads straight to your email, CRM or a Google Sheet.

Do I keep ownership of my accounts?

Yes. Your pages, ad accounts and content stay in your name. We get access as a partner or manager, never ownership.

Can you also edit my listing videos?

Yes. Our video team edits listing tours and agent reels, so your videos and posts come from one team.
