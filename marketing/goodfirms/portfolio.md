# GoodFirms portfolio items

GoodFirms unlocks **Portfolios** only after it approves the listing. When the approval email arrives, open
myaccount.goodfirms.co → Portfolios → Add, and use one entry per project below. Screenshots are in this folder.
All four are Nexus Media's own live projects (no client work, so nothing here needs a client's permission).

## 1. Product Calculator
- **Title:** Product Calculator: costing, inventory and sales app for food businesses
- **Service:** Web Development (also Software Development)
- **Industry:** Food & Beverage
- **Link:** https://nexusmediaph.com/pos/
- **Screenshot:** product-calculator.jpg
- **Description:** A web app that helps Filipino food businesses cost every product, track ingredient stock and see daily sales and profit. Each sale deducts ingredients from stock automatically. It works offline on phone, tablet and computer, installs like an app, and has paid sign-up with online payment and automated follow-up emails to guest users.

## 3. Lead tracking dashboard and WhatsApp AI assistant
- **Title:** Lead capture, tracking and follow-up automation
- **Service:** Software Development
- **Industry:** Startups
- **Link:** https://nexusmediaph.com/our-work/
- **Screenshot:** lead-dashboard.jpg (sample data)
- **Description:** A private dashboard that counts visits to every page without collecting personal data, lists leads from the website and from our WhatsApp AI assistant, and sends personalised follow-up emails with unsubscribe links in one click. The WhatsApp assistant (ElevenLabs conversational AI) answers enquiries in English or Tagalog, collects the client's name, email, service, budget and timeline, and saves each lead to the dashboard automatically.
