## Picture rule: always side by side
- The two stalls stand next to each other on one street. Carla's stall on the LEFT, Jun's on the RIGHT.
- Every beat shows both at the same moment: either one wide shot with both stalls, or a split frame
  (left half Carla, right half Jun) for close-ups. Jun always finishes first and waits; the wait is the joke.
- Carla is never stupid or lazy: she works hard, the spreadsheet is what slows her down.
