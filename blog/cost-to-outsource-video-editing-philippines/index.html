How much does it cost to outsource video editing to the Philippines?

Real 2026 numbers for reels, TikToks, YouTube videos and ads, how Philippine rates compare with the US, UK and Australia, and how to estimate your monthly budget.

By the Nexus Media team, Manila · Updated November 21, 2026

Short answer

Philippine video editing agencies typically charge under 25 USD per hour. US, UK and Australian editors commonly charge somewhere between 40 and 100+ USD per hour, so outsourcing to the Philippines usually costs a fraction of hiring locally. At Nexus Media in Manila, most video projects start from about 1,000 USD (often a monthly batch of reels or YouTube edits), and every project gets a free fixed quote.

What you are actually paying for

Video editing is priced by time. The finished length matters less than how much work goes into it: the amount of raw footage, captions, motion graphics, colour grading and the number of revision rounds. A 30-second reel with animated captions can take longer than a 10-minute talking-head video that only needs cuts.

So the best way to estimate a budget is: hours per video × videos per month × hourly rate. A good agency will turn that into a fixed price per batch so you are never surprised by the invoice.

Philippines vs US, UK and Australia

The work is the same: the same software (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, CapCut), the same platforms and the same trends. What changes is the cost of living behind the rate.

Where the editor isTypical hourly rate
US, UK, Canada, Australia (freelance or agency)about 40–100+ USD
Philippines (agency)under 25 USD

Take a creator who needs 20 reels a month at about 2 hours each. That is 40 hours of editing. At a local rate of 60 USD per hour it costs around 2,400 USD. At a Philippine agency rate under 25 USD per hour it is under 1,000 USD for the same 40 hours.

Freelancer or agency?

A single Filipino freelancer on a marketplace can be cheaper per hour, but you take on the risk: missed deadlines when they are sick, no backup editor, and no one checking quality. An agency costs a little more per hour and gives you a team, a project manager, a fixed quote and someone accountable. For a regular posting schedule, consistency is usually worth more than the last few dollars per hour.

Hidden costs to ask about

  • Revisions: how many rounds are included? At Nexus Media it is two free revisions per video.
  • Music and stock footage: is licensing included, or do you pay for it separately?
  • Turnaround: is 48-hour delivery standard or a rush fee?
  • File transfer and storage: who keeps the project files and raw footage?
  • Payment fees: international transfers and platform fees can add 3–5%.

How to get an exact price

  1. Pick two or three videos you like as a style reference.
  2. Estimate how many videos you need per month and where they will be posted.
  3. Share a sample of your raw footage (a Google Drive or Dropbox link is fine).
  4. Ask for a fixed quote per batch, with turnaround and revisions written down.
